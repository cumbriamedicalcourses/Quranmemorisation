>

>
> Credit line:
> Translation by Talal Itani, ClearQuran.com
>

What this means for the app:

- The translation may be used in the paid course.
- Every place that shows the meanings carries the credit line exactly as given: **Translation by Talal Itani, ClearQuran.com**.
- The text is still shown word for word, never edited.

The licence notice in `itani-clearquran.LICENSE.txt` came with the PDF and, as he says, is out of date. It is kept as a record of what was downloaded.

Keep the original email in the project's records.
